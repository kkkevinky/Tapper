# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Tap with two fingers to right-click, as on a trackpad. The `twoFingerTapEnabled` advanced option turns it off.

## [1.0] - 2026-10-14

First release of Tapper.

- Tap anywhere on the front of the Magic Mouse to left-click, tap twice to double-click, and tap, touch again and move the mouse to drag.
- Light taps count; a finger resting still on the mouse, or a palm on its back, does not block or trigger taps.
- Two-finger swipes switch Spaces, with the Space following the fingers. They work with the palm resting on the mouse, and sideways scrolling is held back during a swipe.
- Pointer Speed slider, Sensitivity Settings window, Launch at Login and Prevent Sleep in the menu bar.
- Two-finger zoom (experimental, off by default).
- Advanced options (tap right-click, swipe style and direction) through `defaults write`; see the README.
